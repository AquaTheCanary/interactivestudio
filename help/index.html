Popular questions

Quick answers to the things creators ask most.

Is Interactive Studio free?

Yes. Interactive Studio is designed to be free to use. There are no paid plans or subscription upgrades in the current product.

How do I sign in?

Open the sign-in page and choose an available sign-in method, or use your email and password. Workspace pages require an authenticated account and will send you to sign-in if needed.

Where do I create a presentation?

Open your workspace and choose New presentation, or go directly to the presentation editor. Your presentation can be saved locally and shared through a presentation room.

Can my audience make choices?

Yes. Add a decision point to a slide, create choices and decide whether each choice jumps to another slide or displays a message.

How do I publish a website?

Create a website, edit its sections and design, save your changes, then open its Publish page. Once published, your site is served from its assigned Interactive Studio address.

Can I use my own domain?

Interactive Studio includes a custom-domain connection flow. You must own or control the domain's DNS settings. DNS changes and certificate issuance can take time to propagate.

Where are my account settings?

Open Settings from your workspace navigation to update your display name, profile picture, public handle and email-verification status.

How do I reset my password?

Use the Forgot password link on the sign-in page and follow the password-reset email instructions.

What if a workspace page gets stuck loading?

Refresh once, check that you are signed in, and try again. If it continues, sign out and sign back in. If only one project is affected, try opening it from the workspace dashboard.

Can I remove the Interactive Studio footer?

Published websites currently include a small Interactive Studio attribution footer with a link back to the platform.

Still need a hand?
