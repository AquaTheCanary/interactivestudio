About the product

What is Interactive Studio?

Interactive Studio is a web-based tool for creating and delivering presentations that go beyond static slides. It brings presentation design, audience participation and presenting tools together in one workspace.

▤

Create presentations

Build slide-based presentations, organise content and shape a clear visual story using the presentation editor.

◇

Add audience interaction

Include decision points that let an audience choose a direction and take part in the experience.

↗

Present and share

Use presenter and audience views to deliver a presentation, then share a presentation link with viewers.

Interactive Studio is operated by Convex, LLC. The service is currently free to use. For product, account or privacy questions, contact support@interactivestudio.abrdns.com.
