Interactive Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Interactive Studio collects, why it is used, how it is stored and shared, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights available to users. Please read it before creating an account or using the service.

In brief: We use account details to sign you in, information and content you submit to provide the presentation service, and technical information to keep the service working and secure. We do not sell personal information or use Google sign-in data for advertising.

1. Who operates Interactive Studio?

Interactive Studio is a web-based presentation and interactive storytelling service operated by Convex, LLC (“we”, “us” or “our”). Convex, LLC determines why and how personal information is processed for the service and is the data controller where applicable data protection law uses that term.

Website: https://interactivestudio.abrdns.com
Privacy and data requests: support@interactivestudio.abrdns.com

2. Personal information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account and profile information: your name, email address, account identifier, profile image (if supplied by your sign-in provider), account type and preferences.
  • Presentation content: presentation titles, slide text, images, layouts, interactive choices, audience responses and other material you create, upload or choose to save in the service.
  • Communications: the contents of messages, feedback or support requests you send to us, together with the contact details needed to reply.
  • Optional AI prompts: prompts, instructions and content that you choose to submit when using an AI-powered feature.

2.2 Information collected when you use the service

  • Authentication and session information: sign-in status, authentication provider, account identifiers and session-related technical information needed to keep you signed in and protect your account.
  • Device and log information: browser and device type, operating system, IP address, request timestamps, pages or features requested, error reports and security events, to the extent made available by our website and infrastructure providers.
  • Preference and storage information: settings and technical records needed to save your preferences and associate your presentations with your account.

3. Google sign-in and Google user data

If you choose “Sign in with Google”, authentication is provided through Firebase Authentication and Google. The sign-in process requests the basic Google account information needed to identify and authenticate you, such as your Google account identifier, name, email address and profile image where available.

  • Why we use it: to create or access your Interactive Studio account, identify your account, display your profile details and maintain your signed-in session.
  • How we use it: Google account information is used for authentication and account management. We do not use Google user data to serve advertisements, sell data, build advertising profiles or determine creditworthiness.
  • What we do not access through Google sign-in: Google sign-in alone does not give Interactive Studio access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Calendar or Google Contacts. We do not request those services as part of the sign-in flow described here.
  • Storage: account details needed for your Interactive Studio account are handled by Firebase Authentication and our application services. Google handles the authentication process under Google's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Sharing: we do not disclose Google user data to third parties except to the service providers needed to operate authentication, when you direct us to do so, or when required by law.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can stop using Google sign-in by choosing another available sign-in method where supported or by contacting us to request account deletion.

Google's privacy information is available at Google Privacy Policy.

4. How and why we use information

  • Provide the service: create and manage accounts, authenticate users, save and display presentations, provide editing and presenting features, and enable sharing or audience participation.
  • Maintain account security: verify sessions, detect suspicious activity, prevent abuse, enforce access permissions and investigate technical or security incidents.
  • Operate and improve the service: diagnose errors, maintain performance, understand feature reliability and develop requested functionality.
  • Provide support: respond to questions, investigate problems and communicate service-related information.
  • Provide optional AI features: transmit the prompt and relevant content you submit to the AI service selected for that feature, so it can generate the requested output.
  • Meet legal obligations: comply with applicable laws, respond to lawful requests and establish or defend legal claims.

5. AI features and third-party AI providers

If you choose to use an AI feature, the text, prompt, instructions or other content you submit may be sent to the relevant AI provider through our service infrastructure to fulfil your request. The provider may process that information under its own terms and privacy policy. AI features are optional; you can avoid sending content to an AI provider by not using those features. Do not submit passwords, payment card details or sensitive personal information in AI prompts.

We do not send your presentation content to an AI provider merely because you have an account. Content is sent when you actively use a feature that requires an AI provider to process it.

6. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

The website and its providers may use browser storage, cookies or similar technologies to maintain authentication, remember preferences, support navigation, protect sessions and make the service function. These technologies may store session identifiers or preference values on your device. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although blocking essential storage may prevent sign-in or parts of the service from working.

7. When we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We may disclose information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: providers that host the website, provide authentication, store application data, protect infrastructure or deliver a feature you request. These may include Firebase and Google services and Cloudflare infrastructure. They process information under their applicable service terms and, where relevant, our instructions.
  • AI providers: only the prompt or content needed when you actively use an AI feature, as explained in section 5.
  • Other users: information or presentation content you choose to share, publish or make available to collaborators or an audience, according to the sharing settings and features you use.
  • Legal and safety reasons: where disclosure is required by law, legal process or a valid authority request, or is reasonably necessary to protect users, the service or legal rights.
  • Business changes: if the service or its assets are involved in a merger, restructuring or transfer, subject to appropriate protections and applicable law.

8. Where information is processed

We and our providers may process information in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, the United States and other countries where their systems or support teams operate. When UK data protection law requires a safeguard for an international transfer, we will rely on an applicable adequacy regulation or an appropriate transfer mechanism, such as approved contractual clauses, as applicable.

9. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, taking account of the type of information and our legal and operational needs.

  • Account information: retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period needed to complete account closure, resolve disputes, maintain security or meet legal obligations.
  • Presentations and saved content: retained while associated with your account or while you keep the content in the service. You may delete content using available product controls; copies in backups or provider systems may take additional time to expire.
  • Authentication and security logs: retained for the period reasonably needed to maintain account security, investigate misuse and troubleshoot the service.
  • Support messages: retained for as long as needed to respond and maintain a record of the support issue.

Retention periods may differ where a provider has its own operational retention schedule or where law requires information to be kept for longer.

10. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing, objection to certain processing, or a copy of information you provided in a portable format. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.

To make a request, email support@interactivestudio.abrdns.com from the email address associated with your account and describe the request. We may need to verify your identity before acting. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. Some rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

If you are in the United Kingdom, you may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

11. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your Interactive Studio account and associated personal information by emailing support@interactivestudio.abrdns.com with the subject “Account and data deletion”. We will verify that the request comes from the account holder, then delete or anonymise information that we no longer need to retain. Some limited records may be retained where required by law, for security, to resolve disputes or to establish or defend legal claims. Information held in backups may be removed in the normal backup rotation cycle.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. These measures include access controls and the security features provided by our infrastructure and authentication providers. No website, transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should use a strong, unique password where password sign-in is available and keep your sign-in credentials confidential.

13. Children's privacy

Interactive Studio is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children who are not legally permitted to use the service in their location without any required parent or guardian involvement. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us at the address above. We will review the request and take appropriate steps where required by law.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the service, our providers, applicable law or our data practices. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when the policy was most recently revised. If a change is material, we will provide additional notice where required by law.
