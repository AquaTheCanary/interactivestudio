2. Information we collect

3. How we use information and our legal bases

  • To create and manage accounts and provide requested features — processing is generally necessary to perform our contract with you or take steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To protect the service, prevent abuse, troubleshoot and maintain security — our legitimate interests in operating a safe and reliable service, balanced against your rights.
  • To respond to support requests — our legitimate interests in helping users and, where relevant, taking steps to perform our contract with you.
  • To meet legal obligations — where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law.
  • For optional activities such as non-essential analytics or marketing — where required, we will ask for your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time.

4. AI features and third-party services

Some features may use external AI services. If you submit a prompt or other content to an AI-powered feature, the relevant content may be sent through our service infrastructure to the AI provider needed to generate a response. Do not submit passwords, payment details, sensitive personal information or anything you are not comfortable sharing with that feature. AI providers process submitted content under their own terms and privacy documentation.

We use service providers to operate Interactive Studio, which may include Firebase/Google for authentication and related infrastructure, and Cloudflare for hosting or request processing. These providers process information on our behalf or as independent providers, depending on the service and their terms.

When you choose Google sign-in, Google also processes information under Google's own privacy policy. We receive the account information needed to authenticate you.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Interactive Studio and its providers may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, maintain security and make the service work. If we introduce non-essential analytics or advertising technologies that require consent, we will provide an appropriate choice before using them.

6. Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with service providers who help us run the website, with professional advisers where necessary, where required by law, or in connection with a genuine business transfer. We only share what is reasonably needed for the relevant purpose.

7. International transfers

Some providers may process information in countries outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we will rely on an applicable adequacy regulation or other permitted safeguard, such as an approved contractual protection.

8. How long we keep information

We keep account information while your account remains active and for a reasonable period afterwards where needed for security, dispute resolution, legal obligations or enforcing our terms. Prompts, support messages and technical logs are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which they were collected. Actual retention periods may vary by provider and data type.

If UK data protection law applies, you may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, restriction of or objection to certain processing, and transfer of information you provided to us. To exercise a right, use the privacy contact above. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. No internet service or method of storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

Interactive Studio is not intended for children who are not legally able to agree to the use of the service in their location. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us using the privacy contact above so we can review it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our service or data practices change. We will update the date at the top and, where required, notify users of material changes.
