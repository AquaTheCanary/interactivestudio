Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you begin.

Can I use Interactive Studio for free?

Yes. Every built-in Interactive Studio feature is free. There are no paid plans, subscriptions or feature tiers.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. Personal accounts support Google sign-in and email/password. Organisation access additionally checks for a verified corporate email address.

How do I get an organisation workspace?

Use the organisation setup request form. Your organisation administrator can then complete the workspace and identity-provider configuration.
