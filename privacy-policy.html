1. Who operates Project?

Project is a web-based presentation creation and audience-interaction service operated by Convex, LLC (“Convex”, “we”, “us” or “our”). Convex, LLC is responsible for deciding the purposes and means of processing personal information for the service and acts as the controller where applicable data protection law uses that term.

Service website: interactivestudio.abrdns.com
Privacy, access and deletion requests: support@interactivestudio.abrdns.com

This policy applies to the Project website, account and presentation features, and associated audience experiences. It does not govern third-party websites or services that you choose to visit or connect; those providers publish their own privacy terms.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account and identity information

When you register or sign in, we may process your name or display name, email address, account identifier, profile image if supplied by your identity provider, authentication provider, account type, sign-in status and account preferences. If you use email and password authentication, authentication systems process the credentials needed to verify your sign-in; we do not display your password to other users.

2.2 Content you create, upload or submit

To provide the presentation service, we process information you choose to place in it. This can include presentation names and descriptions, slide text, uploaded images and other media, layouts, design settings, links, interactive or branching choices, presenter notes, audience prompts, audience selections or responses, and other content you save or submit. Content may include personal information if you choose to include it.

2.3 Communications and support

If you contact us, we process the contact details you provide and the contents of your message, attachments and any follow-up correspondence so that we can respond, investigate the issue and keep an appropriate support record.

2.4 Technical, device and usage information

When you use the website, our hosting, authentication and security providers may make available technical information such as IP address, browser type and version, device or operating-system information, timestamps, requested pages or endpoints, session and authentication events, error details, diagnostic logs and security events. We use this information for service delivery, reliability, fraud and abuse prevention, troubleshooting and security.

2.5 Preferences and local device information

We may process preferences such as theme, editor or presentation settings, and limited values stored in browser cookies or local storage. Browser storage can be used to remember settings, maintain a session or make a feature function correctly.

2.6 Information we do not ask you to provide

We do not need payment-card information to use the free service. Do not submit passwords, payment details, government identifiers or sensitive personal information in presentation content, support messages or AI prompts unless a feature clearly requires it and you have considered whether doing so is appropriate.

3. Google sign-in and Google user data

If you select “Sign in with Google”, Google and Firebase Authentication help verify your identity and return basic account information needed to create or access your Project account. The information may include:

  • Your Google account's unique user identifier.
  • Your name or display name, if available.
  • Your email address and whether the provider indicates it is verified.
  • Your Google profile image URL, if available.
  • Authentication and session information needed to complete sign-in and keep your account authenticated.

3.1 How we use Google user data

We use this information only to authenticate you, identify the correct Project account, create or link your account where supported, display basic account details and maintain account security and sessions. We do not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, use it to build advertising profiles, or use it for credit, lending or eligibility decisions.

3.2 Google services we do not access through sign-in

The Google sign-in flow described here is for authentication. It does not, by itself, grant Project permission to read or manage your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Calendar events, Google Contacts or other Google user content. We do not request those permissions as part of the basic sign-in flow described in this policy. If a separate optional Google integration is introduced in the future, it will be presented separately with its own permission request and explanation before any access occurs.

3.3 Disclosure, storage and retention of Google data

Basic identity information received during sign-in is processed by Firebase Authentication and the Project application to operate your account. It may be processed by the infrastructure providers that host or secure the service, as described in section 9. We do not disclose Google user data to advertisers or data brokers. We disclose it only to providers necessary to operate authentication and the service, when you instruct us to share it, or when disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights and security.

We retain account information while your account remains active and for a reasonable period after a deletion request where needed to complete deletion, resolve disputes, prevent abuse or meet legal obligations. Provider systems may retain limited backup or security records for their ordinary retention periods.

3.4 Google API Services User Data Policy

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, where applicable. We do not use Google user data for advertising, sell it, or transfer it except as necessary to provide or improve user-facing features, for security, to comply with law, or as otherwise permitted by that policy. Google explains its own data practices in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can stop using Google sign-in by using another sign-in method if one is available for your account, or by requesting account deletion using the contact details in section 17.

5. Legal bases for processing

Where the UK GDPR or similar law applies, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases, depending on the particular activity:

  • Contract: processing needed to create and administer your account and provide the features you request.
  • Legitimate interests: operating, maintaining and securing the service; preventing fraud and abuse; troubleshooting; and responding to support requests. We consider your rights and interests when relying on this basis.
  • Consent: where we ask for your permission for an optional activity that legally requires consent. You may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal.
  • Legal obligation: processing needed to comply with applicable law or a lawful request.

6. Presentations, sharing and audience content

Your presentation content is processed to provide the editing, saving, presenting and interaction features you use. Content is not automatically made public merely because you create it. Where a feature lets you publish, share, invite participants or display a presentation to an audience, the information made available depends on the feature and the settings or link you choose.

If you share a presentation or invite an audience, people who can access that presentation may be able to see its content and, depending on the interaction, submit responses. Avoid including personal information about other people unless you have an appropriate reason and permission to do so. You are responsible for reviewing the content and sharing settings before making a presentation available to others.

We may process audience responses and related session information to display or record the interaction you requested. If you are an audience participant, the presenter or organiser may be able to see the response you submit, depending on the feature being used.

7. Optional AI features

Some features may allow you to ask an AI service to generate, rewrite or suggest presentation content. AI use is optional. When you actively use such a feature, the prompt, instructions and relevant content you choose to submit may be sent through our service infrastructure to the AI provider selected for that feature so it can produce a response.

We do not send all of your saved presentations to an AI provider simply because you have an account. Only the information needed for the particular request should be submitted when you invoke the AI feature. The selected provider processes the submitted information under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not submit confidential or sensitive information unless you have reviewed the provider's terms and are comfortable doing so.

8. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

Project and its providers may use essential cookies, browser local storage or similar technologies to maintain authentication and sessions, remember preferences, protect against abuse, support navigation and make the website function. These technologies may store session identifiers, security values or preference settings on your device. We do not use Google sign-in data for advertising cookies or targeted advertising.

You can manage cookies and clear local storage through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies or storage may sign you out or prevent parts of the service from working correctly. Third-party providers may set or access their own technologies when you use their services; their privacy notices explain those practices.

9. How we share information and service providers

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We may share limited information in the following circumstances:

  • Authentication and account services: Firebase Authentication and Google services may process identity and authentication information when you sign in and use your account.
  • Hosting, delivery and infrastructure: Cloudflare and other infrastructure providers may process website requests, IP addresses, technical logs and content needed to deliver, protect or host the service.
  • AI providers: a provider receives the prompt and relevant content only when you actively use an optional AI feature that relies on that provider.
  • People you choose to share with: collaborators, presenters or audience participants may receive content or responses made available through the sharing and presentation features you use.
  • Legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information where required by law or valid legal process, or where reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, our rights or another person's safety.
  • Business changes: information may be transferred as part of a merger, restructuring, financing or sale of the service or its assets, subject to appropriate protections and applicable law.

Service providers are given access only as reasonably needed for their role. Their own terms, privacy notices and processing arrangements may also apply. We do not authorise service providers to use personal information they process for us for their unrelated advertising purposes.

10. International transfers

We and our service providers may process information in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, the United States and other countries where their systems or support teams operate. Where applicable data protection law requires a safeguard for an international transfer, we will rely on an applicable adequacy decision or an appropriate transfer mechanism, such as approved contractual clauses, together with any supplementary measures required in the circumstances.

11. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, taking account of the type of information, how the service is used, security requirements and legal obligations. There is no single retention period for every category.

  • Account and profile information: while your account is active and for a reasonable period after closure to complete deletion, handle disputes, maintain security or meet legal obligations.
  • Presentations and uploaded content: while the content remains saved to your account or is otherwise needed to provide a feature you requested. Deletion from the active service may not immediately remove copies held in backups.
  • Audience responses: while associated with the relevant presentation or session and for as long as needed to provide the feature, maintain records or resolve issues.
  • Authentication and security logs: for the period reasonably needed to protect accounts, investigate misuse, diagnose faults and maintain service security.
  • Support correspondence: for as long as needed to respond and maintain an appropriate record of the support issue.
  • Backups: backup copies may remain until overwritten or deleted through the ordinary backup lifecycle, subject to applicable legal requirements.

12. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether we process your personal information and obtain a copy.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Ask us to delete personal information, subject to applicable exceptions.
  • Ask us to restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Receive certain information you provided in a portable format, where the right applies.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

These rights are not absolute and may be subject to legal conditions or exemptions. We may need to verify your identity and clarify the scope of a request before responding. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

13. Account and data deletion

You may request deletion of your Project account and associated personal information by emailing support@interactivestudio.abrdns.com with the subject line Account and data deletion. Please send the request from the email address associated with your account where possible, and identify the account you want deleted. We may ask for reasonable verification to protect your account from unauthorised deletion requests.

After verification, we will delete or anonymise information that we no longer need to retain, subject to applicable law. Some limited information may be retained where necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or the establishment or defence of legal claims. Copies in backups may persist until the normal backup rotation or deletion process completes. Deleting your Project account does not delete information that you independently shared with other people or information held by third-party services under their own accounts and policies.

14. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against accidental loss and unauthorised access, use, alteration or disclosure. These measures may include authentication controls, access restrictions, secure connections and security features supplied by our infrastructure providers. No website, network transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should use a strong, unique password where password sign-in is available, protect access to your email account and notify us if you suspect unauthorised access.

15. Children's privacy

Project is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children who are not legally permitted to use the service in their location without any required parent or guardian involvement. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us. We will review the report and take steps required by applicable law.

16. Automated decisions

We do not use Google sign-in data to make decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Security systems or service providers may use automated tools to detect suspicious activity, protect accounts or prevent abuse. Where a decision with significant effects is made and applicable law gives you rights relating to it, you may contact us for further information.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service, its features, providers, data practices or applicable laws change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates the latest revision. If a change materially affects how we process personal information, we will provide additional notice where required by law.
